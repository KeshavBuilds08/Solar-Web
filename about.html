Photovoltaic Telemetry & Robotics Architecture

A technical breakdown of the dual-microcontroller embedded control system, telemetry serialization, sensor instrumentation, and autonomous wiper mechanics.

1. The Photovoltaic Soiling Challenge

Dust, airborne particulates, pollen, and industrial soot continuously accumulate on photovoltaic panels. Research indicates that accumulated surface dust can reduce overall power conversion efficiency by 15% to 35% in arid and semi-arid environments.

Traditional manual cleaning requires periodic labor, manual inspections, and significant water consumption. Circuitech introduces an autonomous, sensor-arbitrated closed-loop system that only dispatches mechanical cleaning when soiling breaches calibrated thresholds and atmospheric conditions are optimal.

2. Dual-Microcontroller Distributed Topology

The hardware architecture delegates responsibilities between an IoT communication controller (ESP32) and a real-time motion controller (Arduino Uno R3) to guarantee deterministic motor timings without blocking network communications.

ESP32 Node

Wi-Fi 802.11 Stack, REST API Client, Sensor Ingestion, ThingSpeak Cloud Synchronization

Arduino Uno R3

H-Bridge Driver, Gear Motor PWM, Hardware Limit Switches, Surface Sweep Sequencer

3. Sensor Telemetry & Channel Allocation

Sensors sample continuous telemetry that is serialized into ThingSpeak IoT Channel 3531242:

Telemetry Field Sensor Component Measurement Range Operational Purpose
Field 1 Precision DC Voltage Sensor 0 to 25.0 V DC Measures open-circuit / loaded PV voltage to detect irradiance & soiling drops.
Field 2 ACS712 Current Sensor 0 to 5.0 / 20.0 A Captures instantaneous system amperage for power generation curve calculation.
Field 3 Digital Humidity Sensor 0 to 100% RH Prevents dry mud formation by inhibiting wiping when ambient humidity exceeds 80%.
Field 4 HC-SR04 Ultrasonic Sensor 2 to 400 cm Acoustic distance detection measuring dust accumulation thickness across the glass panel.

4. Electromechanical Actuator & Wiper Gantry

Cleaning is executed by a high-torque 12V DC gear motor coupled with an industrial worm gear transmission and rack-and-pinion sliding gantry.

The wiper assembly utilizes an anti-scratch silicone-rubber squeegee blade. When the ultrasonic sensor detects dust distance below the configured setpoint (default: 10 cm) and relative humidity is below the safety threshold (default: 80%), the system issues an actuation command:

  • Forward traversal at regulated torque across the solar panel face.
  • Reversal trigger via mechanical end-stop limit switch.
  • Return to home position with motor power shutoff to conserve energy.

5. Security & Data Integrity Safeguards

Access to the Circuitech console incorporates military-standard cryptographic standards:

  • Client-Side PBKDF2-SHA256: Passwords are never stored in plaintext. They undergo 100,000 hashing rounds combined with unique 16-byte cryptographically secure random salts.
  • Brute-Force Lockout Engine: 5 consecutive failed authentication attempts automatically lock the account for 5 minutes.
  • Isolated Local Database: Account credentials reside securely in client-side IndexedDB databases protected by the browser's origin boundary.
  • Zero Third-Party Trackers: No external analytics, trackers, or cookies are loaded, ensuring compliance with strict industrial security requirements.
