Engineering Capabilities

Designed for Maximum Photovoltaic Yield

Dust and particulates cause up to 35% degradation in solar array output. Circuitech continuously senses degradation and initiates autonomous recovery.

Real-Time Power Sensing

Continuous DC voltage and current monitoring computes instantaneous wattage to detect yield drops in sub-second granularity.

Ultrasonic Dust Profiling

High-frequency acoustic echo distance metrics verify particle build-up and soiling density directly over the photovoltaic glass.

Atmospheric Safeguard

Integrated humidity sensing checks ambient moisture conditions to prevent mud formation during heavy morning dew or precipitation.

High-Torque Actuation

Arduino-driven DC gear motor actuator smoothly traverses mechanical wiping gantries across the panel surface with automatic limit stopping.

Workflow Automation

How the Closed-Loop Cycle Operates

From hardware sensors to cloud arbitration and physical mechanical cleaning.

01

Multi-Sensor Ingestion

Sensors capture voltage, current, relative humidity, and acoustic distance to establish real-time surface clarity profiles.

02

Telemetry Arbitration

The ESP32 aggregates sensor payloads and streams updates to ThingSpeak channel 3531242 over secure 802.11 Wi-Fi links.

03

Autonomous Actuation

When dust accumulation exceeds thresholds under safe atmospheric conditions, the Arduino Uno executes a motorized sweep sequence.

System Hardware Stack

Integrated Component Ecosystem

Industrial-grade and embedded microcontrollers calibrated for hostile solar environment operations.

ESP32 Microcontroller

Dual-core IoT processor handling Wi-Fi streaming and ThingSpeak API synchronization.

Arduino Uno R3

Dedicated co-processor driving the gear motor relay switches and limit end-stops.

Precision Voltage Sensor

Resistive divider module calibrated to read up to 25V DC panel potential.

Current Sensor Module

Hall-effect analog sensing measuring load current across the inverter rails.

Digital Humidity Sensor

Capacitive relative humidity sensing to inhibit cleaning during rain or heavy dew.

Ultrasonic Range Finder

Sonar transducer analyzing physical dust layering and proximity deviations.

High-Torque DC Gear Motor

12V metallic geared transmission driving the bidirectional wiper gantry.

ThingSpeak Cloud Engine

IoT cloud channel handling real-time data ingestion, aggregation, and JSON feeds.
